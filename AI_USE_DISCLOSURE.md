# AI-use disclosure

I used OpenAI's ChatGPT and Codex to help frame the business decision,
inspect and validate public datasets, design the analytical workflow, and draft
the initial Streamlit implementation and documentation.

I remain responsible for verifying all calculations, joins, visual outputs,
source citations, limitations, and deployment behavior. I reviewed AI-assisted
code by running the app locally, checking representative tract results against
the source CSV, reconciling claims payments to their components, running
index-weight sensitivity tests, and testing the deployed application in a
private browser window.

Known limitations of AI assistance include the possibility of incorrect field
interpretation, coding errors, and overstatement of what a vulnerability index
can establish. The app therefore distinguishes modeled vulnerability from
event probability and does not claim to calculate a final insurance premium.
